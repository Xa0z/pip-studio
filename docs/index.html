Pip Explains

Pip Explains is a TikTok channel about mind-blowing, true facts from space, science, the human body, animals and nature. Every video is hosted by Pip, a small orange robot.

About this app

"Pip Explains" is a small private tool that the channel owner uses to publish Pip's videos to their own TikTok account. It creates short animated science videos and uploads them through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

It is not offered to other people, and nobody else can log in to it.

Contact

Questions about this app: send a message to the Pip Explains TikTok account.

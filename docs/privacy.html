Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the "Pip Explains" app does with data. Short version: it only posts videos to the owner's own TikTok account, and it does not collect data from anyone else.

What the app accesses

  • The basic profile (open ID and display name) of the owner's own TikTok account, to confirm which account is logged in.
  • Permission to publish videos to that same account.

What the app does not do

  • It only posts videos to the owner's own account. It never posts to anyone else's account.
  • It collects no data from other users: no viewers, followers, comments, messages or contacts.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it has no ads or trackers.

How login tokens are stored

The TikTok login tokens of the owner's account are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The encryption key is kept as a private secret and is never published. Tokens are used only to refresh the login and upload videos.

Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. The stored tokens can then be deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of this page shows the latest version.

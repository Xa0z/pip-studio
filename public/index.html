How it works

Setup takes about five minutes in a chat with @PipStudio_bot. After that, videos arrive on schedule.

  1. Connect TikTok

    Log in with TikTok so videos can be posted to your account and their stats read back.

  2. Add a Claude key

    Scripts are written by Claude using your own Anthropic API key, so you control the usage and cost.

  3. Pick a niche and a goal

    Science, history, psychology, money, tech, animals, languages or motivation. Grow followers, get views, or send people to a link.

  4. Choose a host and a voice

    You get three original cartoon characters to choose from, then three voice samples for the one you like.

  5. Set the schedule

    One to three videos a day at times you choose, in your time zone.

  6. Approve and post

    Each finished video comes to Telegram first. Tap Post, ask for a new version, or skip it.

FAQ

Does anything get posted without my approval?

Not in approval mode, which is the default. Every video is sent to you in Telegram first, and only posts when you tap Post.

Can I use a famous cartoon character?

No. Pip Studio only makes original characters and refuses requests for copyrighted or well-known ones, so your channel doesn't get copyright strikes.

Where do the facts come from?

Each script is built around one well-established fact with a named source, and the bot avoids medical, financial and other advice topics that need a professional.

What happens to my data if I stop?

Send /disconnect. TikTok access is revoked and your account, keys, characters, videos and stats are deleted. Videos already on TikTok stay there until you delete them. Details are in the privacy policy.

Which accounts can I connect?

One TikTok account per Telegram user, and only an account you own or manage.

Pip Studio

A Telegram bot that runs a TikTok channel for you. It writes, animates, voices and posts short cartoon explainer videos in your niche, then shows you what works.

Open in Telegram

How it works

  1. Connect TikTok. Log in with TikTok so Pip can post to your account and read your video stats.
  2. Pick a niche and a goal. Space, animals, history, money and more. Grow followers, get views, or send people to a link.
  3. Choose a character and a voice. Pip designs three original cartoon hosts; you pick one.
  4. Set a schedule. One to three videos a day. In approval mode you see every video in Telegram before it posts.
  5. Watch the numbers. /stats, weekly reports and a dashboard show views, likes, followers and which topics and hooks work best.

What Pip does with TikTok

Pip uses TikTok Login Kit to connect your account, the Content Posting API to publish the videos you approved (or scheduled), and the Display API to read your profile, follower count and the views, likes, comments and shares of your videos. It never posts anywhere except your own account, and you can disconnect at any time with /disconnect.
